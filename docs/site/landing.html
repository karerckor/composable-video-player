A FEW THINGS TO TRY

See what the player makes possible.

Each example runs on the same player. The source is right beside it.

01 / FIND

Get to the useful part.

Search chapter titles, jump into a recording, and follow its original captions in a clickable transcript.

Jump to a chapter ↗player.seek(777)
02 / SHARE

Send the exact moment.

A custom Share plugin adds its own control and panel inside the player — including fullscreen and links with a start time.

Try timecoded sharing ↗player.command('openShare', true)
03 / EXTEND

Connect a VAST preroll.

An optional Google IMA plugin pauses content for an ad, honors Skip, and returns to the video after completion or an error.

Try the advertising demo ↗player.command('playPreroll', tag)

The public preview runs in your browser with public Stream media. The deployable example includes the Hono backend. VAST playback is supplied by Google IMA through an example plugin.
